## About
**Chopper's Delight** is an add-on for <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight">Farmer's Delight</a> that adds a variety of new **Cutting Boards**, each crafted from different wood types - including compatibility with popular mods - bringing even more personalization to your Minecraft kitchen!

**I'm still actively developing this mod, so stay tuned for more features!**

</br>

## Features
### 🪵 Cutting Boards for every wood type
Craft a **Cutting Board** out of every wood type in Minecraft—perfect for adding that extra touch of style when decorating your Minecraft kitchen!

### 📜️ Banner patterns
Decorate your **Cutting Board** with **Banner** patterns, giving it a personal touch. Your design is applied to one half of the board, the other half will keep the wooden look. With so many possibilities, you can turn your board into a unique piece of décor that really ties your whole kitchen together.
